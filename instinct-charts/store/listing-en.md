## Full description

```
Instinct Charts is a charting and automated technical analysis app for the Egyptian Exchange (EGX) plus gold, silver and copper. Arabic interface throughout. No account, no subscription, no ads.

INSTRUMENTS
• 36 of the most traded EGX stocks, plus the EGX 30 index
• Gold, silver and copper (COMEX and the London exchange)
• Arabic search that tolerates spelling variants

CHARTS
• Nine timeframes, from one minute to monthly
• Candlesticks with volume, pinch to zoom, drag to pan
• EMA 20/50/200, Bollinger Bands, VWAP
• Indicator panes: RSI, MACD, WaveTrend
• Dark and light themes

AUTOMATED ANALYSIS
Everything below is computed from the candles and drawn on the chart:
• Fair value gaps on the current timeframe, plus daily and weekly gaps overlaid on smaller ones
• Break of structure and market structure shift, with the broken level drawn
• A full Fibonacci grid with the optimal trade entry zone and the equilibrium line
• Order blocks and liquidity pools

CONFLUENCE — WHAT MAKES THIS APP DIFFERENT
A level held up by one reason breaks easily. A level where several independent reasons meet does not. The app gathers candidate levels from eleven sources — Fibonacci ratios, moving averages, prior swings, liquidity, fair value gaps, order blocks, volume-by-price nodes, Bollinger bands, VWAP, previous session extremes and round numbers — then scores each zone.

Crucially, it tests every zone against the instrument's own history: how many times price approached it, how many times it actually bounced, and how many times it broke through. No zone is described as hard to break below a 60% bounce rate, and a zone with many factors but a poor record is labelled as exactly that.

TRADE PLAN
Each instrument gets a written plan: an ideal entry zone, an early entry, a confirmed entry after the break, a stop loss and targets — with risk/reward for each entry and a confidence score backed by a checklist you can read, so you know why the score is what it is.

CANDLE REPLAY
Replay the chart one candle at a time at several speeds. The whole analysis is recomputed on every candle, so you can practise the decision before seeing the outcome.

ALERTS
Alert when price crosses a level, or when it enters an entry zone or a confluence zone — in one tap. Delivered as a notification from your phone.

WORKS OFFLINE
The last downloaded data stays on your device, so the app opens and works without a connection, clearly marked as cached.

PRIVACY
No accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no tracking. Your watchlist, alerts and preferences stay on your device.

⚠️ IMPORTANT
Prices come from a public data provider and are delayed, not real time; Egyptian exchange data is shown at session close. Everything the app shows is automated analysis for educational purposes. It is not investment advice or a financial recommendation. Trading carries the risk of losing your capital. The decision is yours alone, and so is the responsibility.
```
